Privacy Policy for Capybara Battle

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

GREAT TEAM TECHNOLOGY LIMITED ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and share information when you access or use our mobile game, Capybara Battle (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require users to create an account to access its basic gameplay features.

We and our third-party service providers may collect the following information:

  • Device model, operating system, and application version.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers.
  • Gameplay activity and interaction information.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Purchase history and transaction information.
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting customer support.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • Improve gameplay and user experience.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases.
  • Deliver purchased virtual items and game content.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Detect fraud, unauthorized transactions, and technical issues.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. In-App Purchases and Payments

Capybara Battle offers optional in-app purchases, which may include virtual currency, virtual items, game packages, and other digital content.

Purchases made through the Android version of the Game are processed through Google Play Billing.

We do not directly collect or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, payment passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Google processes payment information according to its own privacy policy and applicable payment terms.

We may receive or process transaction-related information, including:

  • Order or transaction identifiers.
  • Purchase tokens and verification information.
  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Purchase date and transaction status.
  • Purchase amount and currency, where available.

This information is used to verify purchases, deliver purchased content, prevent fraud, resolve payment disputes, provide customer support, and comply with legal and accounting obligations.

For information about refunds, please refer to the Google Play refund policy:
